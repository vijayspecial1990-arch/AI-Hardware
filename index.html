Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

The short version
  • No account, email or phone number is required.
  • Your projects, chats, photos and name stay on your phone.
  • Our server keeps only an anonymous device ID, AI usage counts and your subscription status.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads or track you across other apps.

1. Who we are

This policy explains how the AI Hardware Builder mobile app ("the app", "we", "us") handles information. The app helps you plan electronics and maker projects with AI: parts lists, wiring, firmware, build steps and troubleshooting.

Questions about privacy? Email hello@inbound.keyoai.net.

2. No account needed

The app does not ask you to sign up. The first time it opens, our server issues an anonymous device ID and a secret device key. The key is stored in your device's secure Keychain; our server keeps only a one-way hash of it. The device ID is used to count AI usage and link your subscription to this device. It is not linked to your name, email, Apple ID or any other identity.

3. Information stored only on your device

The following stays in the app's private storage on your phone and is never uploaded to or backed up by us:

  • Your projects, parts lists, wiring, code and build progress
  • AI chat history
  • Photos you take or choose for scanning parts and checking build steps
  • The display name you optionally enter on your profile
  • Preferences such as experience level and favourite platforms

Deleting the app removes this information from your phone.

Our server does not store your project content, chats, photos or name.

5. What is sent when you use AI

When you use an AI feature, only the details needed for that answer are sent securely (over HTTPS) to our server and then to our AI provider to generate a response. Depending on the feature, this can include:

  • Your project idea and options (budget, experience, platform, power source)
  • Project context: parts, wiring, build steps and code
  • Your chat message and recent conversation
  • A photo you choose to scan or use to check a build step (compressed first)

This content is processed to produce the answer and is not stored on our server. Please don't include personal information in project ideas, messages or photos.

6. Subscriptions and payments

Subscriptions are purchased through Apple's App Store. Apple processes all payments — we never see or store your card or billing details. To activate your plan we store the App Store transaction ID, product, status and expiry date, and may confirm them with Apple's App Store Server API.

You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime in your App Store account settings. Refunds are handled by Apple.

7. Service providers

We use a small number of providers to run the app. They process data only on our behalf:

  • Supabase — hosts our database and server functions.
  • OpenAI — generates AI answers from the content described in section 5. Under OpenAI's API terms, data sent through the API is not used to train their models by default.
  • Apple — App Store payments and subscription management.

We do not sell, rent or share your information with advertisers or data brokers.

8. Camera and photos

Camera and photo library access are requested only when you choose to scan an item or check a build step. You can change these permissions anytime in iOS Settings. The rest of the app works without them.

9. Tracking, ads and analytics

The app contains no advertising, no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

10. Deleting your data

In the app, go to Profile → Delete all data. This removes every project, chat, photo and your name from your phone, and permanently deletes your device record, usage history and subscription records from our server.

Deleting data does not cancel an App Store subscription — cancel it in your App Store account settings. You can restore an active subscription later with Restore purchases.

You can also email hello@inbound.keyoai.net with the device ID shown in Profile to request deletion or a copy of the data linked to it.

11. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Device keys are stored in the iOS Keychain and only as hashes on our server. Server credentials and AI provider keys are kept on the server and are never included in the app. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect the limited information we hold.

12. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Electronics projects should be carried out with adult supervision where appropriate.

13. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA or the Australian Privacy Act), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your information. Because we don't collect your identity, the simplest way to exercise these rights is the in-app delete option, or email us with your device ID.

14. International processing

Our providers may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States. We rely on their contractual and security commitments to protect it.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. We'll update the date at the top, and for significant changes we will let you know in the app.
